Our Journey

The Arshith Story

Founded in 2019 with the inception of SunTech Solutions, Arshith Group began as an ambitious enterprise dedicated to corporate consulting, operations management, and business scaling. By delivering backend operational support and strategic consulting, SunTech built a strong foundation of trust and client satisfaction.

As service operations expanded into digital consulting and technical infrastructure, all business divisions were consolidated under the unified corporate identity of Arshith Group in 2023—establishing a multi-sector conglomerate engineered for sustainable innovation.

In 2025, the group incorporated Arshith Fresh India Pvt Ltd, pioneering a direct-to-consumer e-commerce platform that connects farmers directly with consumers. By delivering 100% natural, farm-sourced products, Arshith Fresh empowers local agricultural communities while guaranteeing uncompromised purity to households.

Today, through Arshith InfoTech (scaled globally in 2025), the organization engineers cutting-edge software solutions, cloud architectures, and digital transformations for enterprises worldwide. By uniting technology, sustainability, and trust, Arshith Group is building a future where innovation benefits everyone.
